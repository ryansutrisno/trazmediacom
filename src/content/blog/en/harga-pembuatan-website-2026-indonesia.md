Searching for website development cost in Indonesia in 2026 is not as simple as choosing the smallest number in a search result. Two proposals both labelled “company profile website” may differ substantially in design, security, optimisation, and post-launch support. This guide helps SME owners and established companies read market ranges clearly before choosing a vendor.

## 2026 Website Development Cost at a Glance

The ranges below are broad Indonesian market estimates, not binding quotations. The actual price depends on scope, content quality, revisions, integrations, and the team delivering the project.

| Website type | Price range (IDR) | Timeline | Best for |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Landing page | Rp1.5–10 million | 3 days–2 weeks | One product, an ad campaign, or lead generation |
| Basic company profile | Rp4–10 million | 1–3 weeks | SMEs that need a credible online presence |
| Standard company profile | Rp10–25 million | 3–6 weeks | Companies with several services and SEO needs |
| Premium company profile | Rp25–60 million or more | 5–10 weeks | Brands needing custom design and content |
| Online store | Rp15–75 million | 4–10 weeks | Businesses with catalogues, checkout, and order management |
| Custom web app | Rp50 million–hundreds of millions | 2–9 months | Business processes, dashboards, or custom logic |

Use these ranges to form better questions, not to demand a guaranteed result. A Rp1.5 million landing page can be perfectly reasonable when it is a simple page and your copy is ready. A Rp50 million project may also be reasonable if it includes user research, multiple access roles, integrations, quality assurance, and documentation. Ask for a deliverables list before deciding that a vendor is expensive or cheap.

When requesting a quote, separate must-have requirements, useful extras, and work that can wait until after launch. This lets the vendor prioritise without weakening the foundation. Also state who will provide the logo, photography, and copy, because material readiness affects the timeline. If your budget is not fixed, ask for a healthy minimum option and a next-stage option rather than asking for a discount on the same scope.

## 7 Factors That Determine Website Cost

### Design (template vs custom)

A template speeds up production because its visual structure already exists. It can be a sensible choice for a new business that needs a web presence on a limited budget. Custom design requires wireframes, visual direction, and a translation of your brand into every page. The number of design decisions and validation rounds can make it two to three times more expensive than a template approach.

Custom does not automatically mean better. For a new business, money saved on design may be more useful for product photography, copywriting, or advertising. However, when the website is a primary sales tool, a design that matches the audience can reduce uncertainty and guide visitors toward the right CTA.

### Page count and content complexity

Home, About, Services, Portfolio, and Contact pages have different jobs. An effective service page often needs a problem, solution, process, proof, and CTA structure, rather than a short paragraph. Every page must also be written, reviewed, illustrated, and tested on mobile devices.

Ask whether the proposal includes copywriting, image selection, article migration, and product entry. A quote based only on page count can look cheap until content work appears as a separate charge.

### Technology stack (WordPress vs Astro/Next.js)

Technology affects performance, security, and maintenance. WordPress offers an editable CMS and a large plugin ecosystem, but its core, theme, and plugins require regular updates. Astro is a good fit for marketing sites that prioritise lightweight HTML and performance. Next.js is more suitable when a website is becoming an interactive application.

No stack solves every problem automatically. Choose based on how your team works, content needs, integrations, and the vendor’s handover process. For a deeper comparison, read [WordPress vs custom code for business](/en/blog/wordpress-vs-custom-code-untuk-bisnis).

### Features and integrations (payment, WhatsApp API, multilingual)

A basic contact form is very different from a payment gateway with transaction status, notifications, and reconciliation. A WhatsApp button is also different from a WhatsApp API that sends automated messages based on events. Multilingual support adds translation, locale routing, per-language SEO, and content testing.

List integrations during discovery. Mention your CRM, ERP, email marketing platform, payment gateway, and WhatsApp setup. An integration introduced halfway through a project almost always affects its price and schedule.

### SEO and performance (Core Web Vitals, schema, sitemap)

Basic SEO is more than inserting keywords. A vendor may need to configure titles and descriptions, heading structure, sitemap, robots rules, canonical URLs, relevant structured data, and image handling. Performance should be tested on realistic devices and networks, not only on a developer’s laptop.

Core Web Vitals can be useful technical indicators, but a score is not the whole goal. The site must be fast, understandable, and useful to prospective customers. Confirm whether keyword research, schema implementation, and Search Console setup are included.

### What the package includes (domain, hosting, SSL, revisions, handover)

Domains, hosting, SSL, revision limits, and handover details often hide in footnotes. Ask who owns the domain and hosting accounts, how long hosting is included, how many revision rounds you receive, and what assets are delivered. “Free domain” is not very helpful if the vendor registers it in their own name and keeps the credentials.

At minimum, put these items in writing: business-owned domain, active SSL, backups, revision limits, CMS access or source code, design assets, an editing guide, and service credentials. These details reduce the cost and stress of switching vendors later.

### Warranty and after-sales support (maintenance, SLA)

A bug warranty is not the same as a new feature request. A responsible vendor explains the bug-fix period, reporting channel, response time, backups, monitoring, and rates after the warranty ends. For an application supporting daily operations, consider a priority-based SLA.

A small website may only need a warranty period and an hourly support option. A site with transactions or critical integrations needs scheduled maintenance. Estimate this from the beginning rather than waiting for the first incident.

## Ongoing Costs Clients Often Forget

The launch invoice is only one part of total cost of ownership. A domain is renewed annually. Hosting costs can rise as traffic, storage, or backup requirements grow. SSL may be available at no extra charge, but configuration and monitoring still require attention.

WordPress may involve plugin or theme licences, compatibility updates, security work, backups, and monthly maintenance. A custom site can have a higher initial cost, while static hosting and a smaller update surface can make recurring costs easier to predict. Copywriting, photography, illustrations, translation, and video production also belong in the budget if they are not ready.

Ask vendors for a three-year view rather than comparing only the build quote:

| Cost component | WordPress | Custom Astro/Next.js |
|---|---|---|
| Initial build | Often lower for a standard scope | Often higher because design and development are custom |
| Domain and hosting | Annual renewal and configuration-dependent capacity | Domain renewal and deployment/hosting as needed |
| Plugins or services | Can accumulate every year | Usually fewer for a marketing site |
| Maintenance | Updates, backups, security, compatibility | Dependency updates, monitoring, and feature changes |
| Three-year cost | Low upfront; add licences and support | Higher upfront; often more predictable |

Neither column is always the cheapest. Include your internal team’s time as a cost. An editable CMS can save developer hours, while a simpler system can save maintenance hours.

## How to Read a Vendor Proposal (Red Flags & Green Flags)

**Red flags** deserve further questions:

- An unusually low price with no page, feature, or revision breakdown.
- A vendor that never asks about your business goal, customers, or workflow.
- A portfolio unrelated to the type or complexity of project being proposed.
- Unclear ownership of the domain, hosting, design files, or source code.
- Promises made in chat but absent from the proposal or contract.

**Green flags** are visible in the process, not in jargon:

- A discovery call happens before a solution and price are prescribed.
- Scope, assumptions, exclusions, milestones, and acceptance criteria are written down.
- Payments follow milestones rather than being fully upfront without protection.
- Source code, assets, access, and documentation are included in handover.
- The vendor explains performance, SEO, feature, and budget trade-offs plainly.

## Choosing the Right Payment Model

**Fixed price** works when scope is clear and changes are limited. You receive budget certainty while the vendor carries estimation risk. Make sure out-of-scope work has an agreed change-request process and rate.

**Time and materials** bills for the time and material actually used. It is flexible for discovery, an MVP, or a product that is still changing. In return, you need a prioritised backlog, time reports, and a budget ceiling so costs do not run without control.

**A retainer** buys recurring capacity or hours. It is useful for maintenance, ongoing optimisation, content work, and small enhancements. Clarify whether hours expire, how tickets are prioritised, the response time, and what work is excluded.

## Why a Professional Software House Costs More Than a Freelancer

A software house quote covers more than one developer’s coding hours. It may include project coordination, design, QA, code review, documentation, and backup capacity when a team member is unavailable. This often makes the initial cost higher, but reduces dependence on one person for a complex project.

A freelancer can be an excellent choice for a simple landing page, especially when scope and materials are ready. For a system with several integrations or long-term support, compare risk and total cost, not just the first invoice. ## FAQ

### What is the minimum cost to build a website in Indonesia in 2026?

A landing page starts at around Rp1.5 million in the market. Trazmedia starts from Rp2.5 million, including a domain, one year of hosting, SSL, and basic SEO optimisation. Check the package carefully: “starting from” may not include copywriting or unlimited revisions.

### Why can website prices differ five to ten times between vendors?

Because scope, technology, and after-sales support can differ dramatically. A Rp500,000 website is usually a ready-made template without optimisation, while a Rp5 million website may be custom-built and SEO-optimised. Those amounts illustrate a market pattern; ask for the work breakdown before comparing them.

### Does a website have to use WordPress?

No. WordPress is useful for content-heavy sites and non-technical teams. For a landing page, company profile, or web app focused on performance and SEO, Astro or Next.js may be a better fit.

### Can I negotiate with a software house?

Yes, as long as the scope remains clear. Common negotiating points are the timeline, including an express fee, revision rounds, or bundled maintenance. Avoid cutting QA, security, and documentation simply to lower the quote.
